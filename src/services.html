Digital & On-Site Library Services

Explore our digital resources, study spaces, and self-service facilities available to registered members and city residents.

1. Online Public Access Catalogue (OPAC)

Search over 45,000 print and digital titles, view real-time shelf availability, and place holds from home.

  • Timings: Available 24/7 online
  • Steps: Sign in with your Library Card ID → Search by title or ISBN → Click Reserve Copy.

2. E-Books & Digital Audiobooks

Borrow downloadable EPUB/PDF e-books and narrated audiobooks on your phone, tablet, or e-reader with automatic returns.

  • Timings: 24/7 instant download (14-day digital loan)
  • Steps: Log in with an active membership → Select up to 3 titles → Read online or download for offline use.

3. Study-Room Booking

Reserve quiet individual focus pods or 4-seat group study rooms equipped with whiteboards and power outlets.

  • Timings: Monday – Saturday, 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM (2-hour slots)
  • Steps: Book online up to 48 hours in advance → Check in at the 2nd-floor help desk within 15 minutes.

4. Printing, Scanning & Photocopying

Self-service network kiosks for black-and-white or color document printing and high-speed PDF scanning to USB or email.

  • Timings: Monday – Saturday, 9:30 AM – 7:30 PM
  • Steps: Upload files at the Print Station → Tap your library card → Collect prints (document scanning is free).

5. Free Public Wi-Fi & Research Terminals

Complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi across all reading halls plus dedicated desktop stations for academic databases.

  • Timings: Daily during library hours (8:30 AM – 9:00 PM)
  • Steps: Connect to ReadersHaven-Guest → Enter your daily access code from the front desk.
